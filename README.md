# Commute vs Rent

A static web page that answers "is a higher rent worth a shorter commute?" with one number: what each hour saved costs you. It takes both rents, both commute lengths (round trip, per working day), working days per month, optional commute costs (gas, transit, parking), and optionally what an hour of your free time is worth to you. Nothing leaves your browser.

**Problem (Reddit):** [How much can I reasonably increase my rent to reduce commute?](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/comments/1y4bq8x/) (r/personalfinance): 2.5 hours of commuting a day, rent of 2,700 a month, and the question of how much more is reasonable. The tool does not pick a number for you. It shows the price of each hour you get back, and you compare it with what your time is worth.

## Use
Open `index.html` in a browser. No install.
